# Privacy Policy for "No Chivas"

"No Chivas" is a local-only utility application designed to help players keep track of their Domino game scores. We take your privacy seriously. 

We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data.

The application operates entirely offline. All data used within the app is stored completely locally in memory on your device, and is cleared when the game is reset. We do not use third-party analytics, diagnostic tools, or advertising frameworks that track your usage. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, this application is completely safe for children of all ages to use.
